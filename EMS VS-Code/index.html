Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

What 3D software and rendering engines do you utilize?
Our pipeline primarily leverages Autodesk Maya, Blender 4, Houdini FX, and Unreal Engine 5.4 with real-time Lumen and Nanite rendering. For post-production, we rely on Adobe After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, and Foundry Nuke.
How does your project milestone and revision cycle work?
We follow a structured 4-phase approval cycle: Storyboard/Animatic sign-off, Rough 3D Layout review, Textured/Lighting preview, and Final 4K Render mastering. Standard contracts include up to 2 major revision iterations per phase.
Can Paoyaila build custom enterprise ERP and EMS portals?
Yes! In addition to digital animation, we design and engineer full-stack Employee Management Systems (EMS), payroll engines, multi-tiered role authentication portals, and real-time database integrations.
Do you offer internship programs for aspiring 3D artists?
Absolutely. We run dedicated 3-month and 6-month intensive internship programs with direct mentorship from senior TDs and animators. Visit our Careers Portal to apply!
